Floodwater is almost always rated Category 2 (gray water) or Category 3 (black water), so it carries bacteria, contaminants and possibly hazardous material that can make people seriously ill. For flood emergencies anywhere in Dunwoody, Dunwoody Restoration is available 24/7 to extract the water, disinfect affected areas and bring the property back safely.

Anything unsalvageable comes out and is hauled off correctly, including wet insulation, drywall that took in contaminated water and waterlogged carpet padding.

Warning Signs for Flood Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • An overflowing washing machine, dishwasher or water heater
  • Water collecting along the foundation because the yard is saturated
  • Water coming in through foundation cracks, window wells or door thresholds
  • A sump pump that failed during heavy rain and let the basement flood
  • A sewer or drain backup pushing contaminated water into living spaces
  • Mud, silt or debris left on floors once the water goes down
  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through

What a Professional Brings to Flood Cleanup

There is much more to a flood cleanup than removing the water you can see. Chemicals, bacteria, sewage and other contamination ride in with the flood and create real health dangers. Professional flood crews are trained in containment, personal protective equipment (PPE) and sanitizing protocols that keep your family from exposure to pathogens such as E. coli, hepatitis and other waterborne diseases.

Improper cleanup also tends to miss moisture you cannot see, and within days that trapped water can cause a major mold problem. Professional technicians use moisture meters, thermal imaging and psychrometric calculations to make sure every affected area, including wall cavities, subfloors and framing, reaches an acceptable drying standard. This scientific approach to drying is the only reliable way to prevent secondary damage that can cost thousands more to fix.

How to Prevent Flood Cleanup Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Elevate appliances and electrical systems in flood-prone parts of your property
  • A battery-powered backup sump pump keeps water moving if the electricity fails in a storm
  • Look into a flood insurance policy, including when your home sits outside a mapped flood zone
  • Regrade low spots so soil around the whole foundation falls away from the walls
  • Roof runoff should leave the downspout far enough out that it cannot soak the foundation
  • Clear leaves and debris from storm drains and gutters, which lets water drain the way it should
